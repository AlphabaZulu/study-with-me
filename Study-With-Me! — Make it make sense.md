About us

# Make it make sense.

Study-With-Me! exists for one reason: to make difficult academic concepts feel simple, clear and doable.

✳ aha!

Our mission

## Big ideas. Simple words.

Some topics feel impossible the first time you meet them. Not because you can’t understand them, but because they’re often explained in a way that doesn’t click. Study-With-Me! takes those tricky concepts in Accounting, Economics, Business Management and Law and breaks them down into plain language, relatable examples and small steps.

Through educational videos, study notes, flashcards and practice questions, the goal is simple: help you understand, not just memorise, so you can walk into a test or exam feeling ready.

*✧***Clarity first**Plain language and real-life examples before jargon.

*◷***At your pace**Bite-sized lessons you can fit around your day.

*♡***A calmer way**Less overwhelm, more “oh, now I get it.”
