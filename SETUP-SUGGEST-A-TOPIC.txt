STUDY-WITH-ME! — SWITCHING ON THE "SUGGEST A TOPIC" FORM
=========================================================

The form sends students' suggestions to your inbox through Formspree.
It stays switched off until you add your Formspree endpoint.

1. CREATE THE FORMSPREE FORM (about 5 minutes, free plan)
   a. Go to https://formspree.io and sign up using wstudy585@gmail.com.
      Choose the free plan. No card is needed.
   b. Open the verification email from Formspree in that Gmail inbox and
      click the link. Check the spam folder if you can't find it.
   c. In Formspree, click "+ New Form". Name it "Suggest a Topic" and
      choose wstudy585@gmail.com as the email address to send to.
   d. Copy the form endpoint. It looks like:
         https://formspree.io/f/abcdwxyz

2. PASTE THE ENDPOINT INTO THE WEBSITE
   Open  data/config.js  and put the endpoint between the quotes:
         suggestFormEndpoint: 'https://formspree.io/f/abcdwxyz'
   Save the file. Nothing else needs to change.
   (The endpoint is a public form address, not a secret key.)

3. PUBLISH ON GITHUB PAGES
   a. Upload everything in this folder to your GitHub repository, keeping
      the data folder as it is.
   b. In the repository, go to Settings > Pages and set the Source to
      "GitHub Actions" (see SETUP-NEWS-FEED.txt, steps 2 to 4).
   c. Wait a minute or two, then open your GitHub Pages address.

4. SEND A TEST SUGGESTION (please don't skip this)
   a. On the live site, click "Suggest a Topic" and send a test.
   b. The first submission may ask you to confirm the form in Formspree.
      Follow the email or dashboard prompt if it does.
   c. Check that wstudy585@gmail.com receives an email with the subject
      "Study-With-Me! — New Student Request" (check the spam folder too).
      If it lands in spam, mark it "Not spam".
   Email delivery only counts as working once this test arrives.

5. RECOMMENDED FORMSPREE SETTINGS
   - Keep Formspree's spam filtering switched on.
   - Free plan limit: 50 submissions a month. Extra submissions are held
     back until the next month or until you upgrade. Don't upgrade
     without deciding you want to pay.

Note: "Study together" rooms only work while the site is hosted on
claude.ai. On GitHub Pages that section will show as unavailable.
Everything else works as a normal website.
