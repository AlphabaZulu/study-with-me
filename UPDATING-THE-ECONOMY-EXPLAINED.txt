STUDY-WITH-ME! — KEEPING "THE ECONOMY EXPLAINED" UP TO DATE
==========================================================

HOW IT WORKS NOW
The news section reads one file: data/economy.js. The Rate Watch and the
article indicator boxes read their figures from data/pulse.js (the same
file as the Economic Pulse ticker), so the two never disagree.
There is NO live news feed. The section is labelled "In Development".

ADDING OR UPDATING AN ARTICLE (by hand)
1. Find the official release (SARB, Stats SA, National Treasury) or a
   reputable news report.
2. In data/economy.js, copy an article block and change every field.
   - headline: the publisher's headline, word for word
   - summary and explanations: in your own words (never paste paragraphs)
   - facts: confirmed figures, each with its source
   - forecasts: what named experts expect (not facts)
3. If a figure changed, update data/pulse.js too.
4. After each MPC decision, update "rateWatch" in data/economy.js and the
   repo and prime figures in data/pulse.js.

THE DAILY DATA CHECK (GitHub Actions — not yet tested on GitHub)
Included: .github/workflows/data-check.yml and tools/check-freshness.mjs
- Every morning it checks data/economy.js for mistakes (missing sources,
  impossible dates, unknown categories).
- It opens one GitHub issue, "Data due for update", when a Pulse figure
  passes its review date or an MPC decision date has passed.
- It never changes the website itself and needs no API keys.
To switch it on: upload the whole folder (including the hidden .github
folder) to your repository. In the repository's Actions tab, open
"Data check" and press "Run workflow" once. Check that it runs green.
You can also run it on your own computer:  node tools/check-freshness.mjs

WHY NOT A FULLY AUTOMATIC NEWS FEED?
News APIs that allow republishing headlines usually need a paid plan and
a secret key (which must live on a server, never in the website). Even
then, every explainer still needs to be written and checked by a person.
The daily check tells you WHEN to update; you decide WHAT to publish.
